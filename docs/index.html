What this site is

A high-level framing layer. It helps visitors decide whether they are aligned, and where to engage (sponsor, contributor, exemplar, partner).

What it is not

Not a policy manifesto and not a “perspective maze”. Deep worldview content lives behind optional links, not the primary on-ramp.

Club signal

We treat this as an invite to exemplars. The exemplars page can list “already aligned” orgs and thinkers, plus a clear “how to join” pathway.

Seed-phase promise

Keep it simple: show the mission, show credibility cues, show concrete asks, and provide a low-friction path to talk.
