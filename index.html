Evidence before allegiance.

When every issue seems connected, it is easy to confuse a compelling story with a documented fact. CoPolitic is a proposed research entry point for checking claims, comparing explanations and preserving the right to disagree.

No party affiliation or political belief is required to examine the sources.

A three-step test

Make the reasoning inspectable.

  1. Separate. Identify the original claim, evidence, opinion and uncertainty.
  2. Compare. Read primary sources and serious competing explanations before drawing conclusions.
  3. Challenge. Ask what evidence could change the conclusion; keep corrections and unanswered questions visible.

No result earns authority from repetition, popularity or AI confidence alone.
